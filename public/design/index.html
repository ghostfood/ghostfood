Dual Group

Go to Dual's My Legal Indemnity Shop homepage (since redesigned), or browse this slideshow demonstrating its initial design process

Mock-ups and CSS were developed quickly working from site map flowcharts and a working prototype of the product built with Bootstrap

A bright and bold look and feel was deemed appropriate for a UX process heavy on form selection. The user's journey through the quote process was displayed as a five stage navigation.

The product made extensive use of customised Bootstrap components, webfonts and FontAwesome for iconography. Additions and updates were controlled through GIT (via Bash terminal), SourceTree and Bitbucket.

Different brands were able to adopt the product with bespoke stylesheets to reflect their identity.

Conceptual artwork was quickly devised, incorporating photography on Wikimedia, to serve as background imagery for region-specific versions of the product
