UK Chart hits
1952-2019

Based on data presented on Statjam.org's At A Glance: UK Chart Hits. Playlists are ordered by the week chronologically and then by the position the release entered at (in ascending order e.g. from #1 to/up to #100) within the chart that week.

How a chart entry is defined

Chart entries are defined as each entry as listed in the 'official' charts published at the time.

For example, a double A-side single is one entry, an EP release is one entry and a re-entry that peaked at 75 or higher one or more years after its original chart appearance is one entry.

How playlist eligibility is defined

The maximum total for each playlist is the quantity of individual chart entries plus any 'AA sides' and one additional track from each single listed as an EP release. Re-entries within the same year of the release's original chart entry are excluded, as are re-entries that did not peak at 75 or higher on the chart.

How playlist totals are defined

The current playlist totals indicate whether the correct number of entries has been applied to the playlist, including all 'silent track' placeholders, in anticipation of a playlist with all eligible tracks accounted for and playable.

How missing or omitted entry totals are defined

The 'missing or omitted' entries total is the current amount of 'Silent track' (or equivalent) placeholder entries in the playlist, whether because those entries are not available on the service, ARE available but have not yet been added to the playlist, or have been omitted due to being a recurring Christmas-related re-entry.

How else re-entries may be excluded from the dataset and playlists

Re-entries occurring in January may be excluded from the playlists if they had only ended their prior chart run in December of the previous year. Exceptions are made if the track entered the Top 75 for the first time after re-entry or re-entered the Top 75 later that same year.
