About these mixes

Poptimix 00 was first published in March 2009 and intended as a supplementary bonus to the proposed Ultramix 00.

Ultramix 00 parts 1-4 were originally published in April 2009. Since then Parts 1 and 2 have been combined with several minor alterations made to them and the old Part 4 (now Part 3).

A new Part 4 was published in July 2011. Part 5 was published in October 2011 and includes a segment from Poptimix 00. Parts 6 and 7 eventually appeared in April 2012 and parts 8 and 9 were finally published in May 2012.
