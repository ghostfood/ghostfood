About these mixes

Poptimix 07 was created and published in December 2007. This is a shorter version of a standard Ultramix hence the alternate title. Originally this edition was released as seven shorter chapters but has since been reworked into just two longer ones. Eventually there will be an expanded Ultramix 07 edition featuring more tracks but not until all other editions in the series have been completed.
