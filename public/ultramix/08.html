About these mixes

Ultramix 08 was completed and published in December 2008 and January 2009. Parts 3 and 4 were later merged in March 2011 with some minor re-editing, resulting in a revised nine part edition.

Kylie Minogue, Janet Jackson and Madonna all appear on both the first and penultimate mixes in the series chronologically. Q-Tip achieves this but not as a solo artist having appeared as a group member of A Tribe Called Quest and a guest vocalist (with Deee-Lite) on Ultramix 90. Guru Josh achieves this but only due to his one appearance on Ultramix 90 re-appearing in remixed form on this year's mix.

Grace Jones is the longest-active artist to appear (and debut) on Ultramix 08 with a pop career spanning at least 30 years but Madonna, Janet Jackson and now Cyndi Lauper (also making her first Ultramix appearance) are not far behind with 25 year+ careers. Kraftwerk would better this record by virtue of appearing on Ultramix 03 however. Madonna has now appeared on eight different Ultramixes to date.
