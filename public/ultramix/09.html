About these mixes

Ultramix 09 parts 1-12 created and published between August 2009 and August 2010. This is the final release in the Ultramix series chronologically and will likely be the only one to exceed ten hours in total length.

Some connections between the first and final mixes in the series include the song 'Dirty Cash' by The Adventures Of Stevie V, later appropriated by Dizzee Rascal as 'Dirtee Cash', appearing on both. Similarly, Kid 606's 'Mr Wobble's Nightmare' is based on 4 Hero's 'Mr Kirk's Nightmare'. But Beltram's 'Energy Flash' could be considered the only track to appear on more than one Ultramix (with an 18 year gap between the two) in only its original form and a re-edited form of the original (as opposed to an official remix).

Artists who appear on both the 90 and 09 Ultramixes are Pet Shop Boys, Beltram, Saint Etienne, Andrew Weatherall, Depeche Mode and, as featured guests only, De La Soul.

Artists appearing who also debuted on Ultramix 91 are Busta Rhymes (then as a member of Leaders Of The New Skool), The Prodigy, Doves (then as Sub Sub) and U2. Jan Hammer rivals Grace Jones for longest-active artist to appear on an Ultramix, albeit with a sole remixer credit.
