About these mixes

Ultramix 90 parts 1-5 created and published between August 2009 and April 2010, based on and superseding the original 9 part version created and published in January 2005. The 2010 edition features reworked and re-structured sequences, extra tracks and a new ending - see the tracklist for details.

A selection of acts featured on the mix had been active and/or enjoying chart success for several years before 1990 - particularly The Fall, Depeche Mode, The B-52's, Was (Not Was), INXS, Madonna, Yazoo (here with a remix of an older hit), Janet Jackson, Cabaret Voltaire, Pet Shop Boys, The Cocteau Twins and New Order (here as EnglandNewOrder). By contrast a total of 66 artists (including remixers, solo artists previously in featured groups and some aliases of previously featured artists) appear with their debut single or a single released before their most successful period.
